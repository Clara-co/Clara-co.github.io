Data integrity flags — resolve before presenting

Critical · News Corporation: Advisory coding contradicts own evidence
Nature of Comm Role = Advisory but the Direct Quote/Evidence field reads "No mention of communications or reputation professionals in any governance document reviewed" and the binary Comm Y/N = N. This is a three-way internal contradiction. Should be None. Not fixed in v4 — requires correction before any Advisory count is reported.
Needs PI adjudication · Google/Alphabet — "participate" ≠ voting
Evidence: "VP of Global Affairs and Chief Privacy Officer participate in AI deployment reviews." The word "participate" does not meet the codebook's Voting definition. Chief Privacy Officer is not a communication/reputation professional. Candidate for downgrade to Advisory.
Needs PI adjudication · Meta — LeCun is a technical, not comm, professional
Evidence leads with Chief AI Officer Yann LeCun (technical role), then notes "VP of Global Affairs sits on AI governance review panels." "Sits on panels" does not confirm voting authority. PI must rule whether VP of Global Affairs qualifies as a comm/reputation professional under the codebook.
Needs PI adjudication · Accenture — "dissemination" is functional, not governance
Evidence: "communications and brand teams participate in Responsible AI principles dissemination." Disseminating approved principles is a communications function, not a governance voting role. The evidence note is also cut off mid-sentence in the source data. Candidate for downgrade to Advisory.
Methodological · 0 of 50 PI reviews complete
PI Review Complete = N for every row. Peer reviewers at HBR-level outlets will ask for this. The codebook calls PI review non-optional. At minimum, the 7 Voting rows and 1 Advisory contradiction should be reviewed before public presentation of findings.
Analytical · Sector confound unresolved in voting score gap
The 19.0 vs 11.5 score gap between voting and non-voting companies cannot currently be attributed to comm role — all 7 voting companies are Technology sector, which also scores highest overall (17.1 avg). The Priority Queue Groups 1–2 must be coded to test whether comm role predicts governance quality independent of sector before this comparison is presentation-ready.
