The moment you click.
When you search online, your device ID and location are logged automatically.
Advertisers bid on your information in an automated auction that runs before the page even loads.
Data brokers purchase it and build a detailed profile of you. You are never notified.

7
Slow it down.
1
Audit app permissions. In your device settings, turn off location access and ad tracking for apps that do not need them.
2
Use email aliases. Using an alias or temporary email can stop companies from linking your different accounts back to your real identity.
3
Choose data-secure apps. Look for apps and websites that are transparent about how they store and use your data.
Every layer you add makes you less profitable as a data source.
